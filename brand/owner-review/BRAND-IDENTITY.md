### 1.1 Brand roles
| Level | Brand | Role | Visual treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Master | **Eco Nojin** | Ecosystem umbrella; trust, science, land | Primary logo; teal+sand |
| Sub-brand | **HyDroMA** | Scientific engine; intelligence, modeling | Indigo; geometric; "Powered by" relationship |

- **HyDroMA** is always presented as **"HyDroMA — the scientific engine of Eco Nojin"** (never as an independent product).
- **Nojin Marketplace** is **"Nojin Marketplace — part of Eco Nojin"**.
- Sub-brand logos never replace the master logo in global navigation; they appear within their scoped contexts.

## 2. INTERNATIONAL POSITIONING

### 2.1 Core idea
**"Living Territories, Intelligently Managed."**

Eco Nojin connects **science, land, water, livelihoods, commerce, and finance** into one ecosystem for the restoration and sustainable management of living territories worldwide.

### 2.2 Brand pillars
| Pillar | Meaning | Expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Living Territories** | Land, water, soil, communities as living systems | Land/water motifs, not just "green" |
| **Scientific Intelligence** | Evidence-based decisions (HyDroMA) | Data-driven, precise, validated |
| **Restoration & Resilience** | Healing land, climate resilience | Growth/restoration gradients |
| **Sustainable Livelihoods** | Farming, nomadic, rural prosperity | Warm, human, commerce |
| **Open Ecosystem** | Interoperable, international, multi-tenant | Modular, standards-based |

### 2.3 What the brand is NOT
- Not a single-country agricultural brand.
- Not an "eco/leaf/green" cliché brand.
- Not a single-product brand.
- Not a political or religious brand.

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY

### 3.1 Logo concept
**Eco Nojin logo:** A rounded square "territory" tile containing:
- A **deep teal sky/land** field (Territory Teal).
- A **flowing cyan water line** (Water Cyan) — representing water, data, and flow.
- A **sand/amber sun node** (Living Sand) — representing land, harvest, and energy.
- The composition evokes a **living landscape seen from above** (land + water + sun), not a literal leaf.

**HyDroMA logo:** A hexagon (scientific/molecular) with **flow lines and nodes** — representing data flow, modeling, and scientific rigor. Indigo + cyan.

**Nojin Marketplace logo:** An abstract **"N" / market arch** in coral — representing trade, connection, and exchange.

### 3.2 Logo files
- `assets/eco-nojin-logo.svg`
- `assets/hydroma-logo.svg`
- `assets/nojin-marketplace-logo.svg`

### 3.3 Logo usage rules
- **Clear space:** ≥ 1× the logo's corner radius on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 24px (digital), 8mm (print).
- **Do not:** stretch, recolor outside palette, add effects, rotate, place on busy backgrounds without a scrim.
- **On dark:** use white/cyan variant.
- **On light:** use primary teal variant.
- **Co-branding:** "HyDroMA" + "Eco Nojin" lockup with a divider; HyDroMA never appears alone in global nav.

### 3.4 Color system
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Territory Teal | `#0B5563` | Primary, headers, brand |
| Living Sand | `#C9A227` | Accent, highlights, harvest |
| Water Cyan | `#35C4D7` | Data, water, interactive |
| Science Indigo | `#2E3A87` | HyDroMA sub-brand |
| Market Coral | `#E76F51` | Marketplace sub-brand |
| Ink | `#0F1E24` | Text, dark surfaces |
| Slate | `#5A6B72` | Secondary text |
| Cloud | `#F5F7F8` | Backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, text on dark |

**Rule:** Green is **not** a primary brand color. Green appears only as a **semantic** color (success/vegetation) in data visualization.

### 3.5 Typography
- **Persian/Arabic:** Vazirmatn (open source, SIL OFL) — RTL.
- **Latin:** Inter (open source, SIL OFL).
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono (data/code).
- Scale: display 2.5rem / h1 2rem / h2 1.5rem / h3 1.25rem / body 1rem / small 0.875rem / caption 0.75rem.

### 3.6 Iconography
- **Style:** 2px stroke, rounded caps, geometric.
- **Set:** Lucide (open source, ISC) as base; custom scientific icons (water drop, soil layers, model, satellite) where needed.
- **Rule:** icons never carry meaning alone; always paired with a label.

### 3.7 Graphic patterns
- **Topographic lines** (subtle, low-opacity) — land/terrain motif.
- **Flow lines** (water/data) — animated or static.
- **Grid mesh** (scientific) — for data-heavy surfaces.
- Avoid: leaf patterns, recycling symbols, earth-in-hands clichés.

### 3.8 Imagery
- **Photography:** real landscapes, real people (farmers, nomads, scientists), natural light, documentary style.
- **Illustration:** isometric/geometric for scientific concepts; flat for UI.
- **Data visualization:** accessible, colorblind-safe, consistent.

### 3.9 Scientific data visualization style
- **Validated** = green `#2E9E5B`, **Calibrated** = amber `#D98A1F`, **Uncalibrated** = gray `#8A97A0`, **Not Validated** = red `#D64545`.
- Always show provenance + validation state.
- No fabricated data; DEMO labels for samples.
